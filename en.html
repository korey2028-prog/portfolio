TEM-4 Study Workspace

After answering a drill, what should you practice next?

During exam preparation, my practice questions, answer keys, and error notes were scattered across notebooks and web tabs. I consolidated them into a unified desk: answer questions, submit to see answers and grammatical breakdowns, and automatically route errors to a review deck. Later, I replaced hardcoded dates with customizable target scores, closing the loop from practice to review. Official TEM-4 scores rose from 53 to 69 during use.

02 iOS PRODUCT · WEB PROTOTYPE · HEALTH

Fitness OS

After logging workouts and meals, how do you easily review the trend?

Both in my own training and when coaching gym members, I needed to review workouts, nutrition, and subjective fatigue in one place. I built an app around that workflow: enter raw notes, verify the parsed date and content before saving, and revisit history on a clean timeline. Unknown values are never stored as zero. The native app pairs with Apple Health to suggest minimal daily actions, supported by an interactive web demo for zero-friction evaluation.

03 AI WORKFLOW · LIVE OPS

DM Rehearsal & Role Handbook

Before stepping on stage, how do you see at a glance who to cue, what to do, and which line to speak?

While working as an immersive role-play game host (DM), I compiled a 132-page rehearsal manual for deep backstage prep and a 34-page live runbook for on-stage cue reading. Information is structured by act into entrance signals, physical actions, and exact lines. The venue owner shared the guides with other hosts as standardized reference. Complex narrative material compressed into an actionable live index.

HOW I WORK

How I turn raw friction into practical tools

01

Start on the floor

When coaching, studying, or performing, note the exact step where things grind to a halt before deciding on features.

02

Know who uses it

Focus the screen on immediate needs. Grounded in practical validation: where attention comes from, who finds real value, and how the first loop holds up.

03

Let AI write the drafts

Write clear scopes, protect essential content, and specify acceptance checks before handing execution to AI coding tools.

04

Test it with my own hands

A page opening without errors is just the beginning. I walk through the flow step-by-step as an actual user, checking edge cases, empty states, and save behavior.

05

Be honest on progress

Running locally, public demo, tested on device, and validated by users are distinct stages of maturity.
